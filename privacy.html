Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Policy explains what personal information the Marketplace collects, why we use it, who may receive it, where it may be processed and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Who is responsible

Nyandwi Land Marketplace (“Marketplace”, “we”, “us”) operates nyandwi-land.pages.dev and determines how personal information is used for this service. We are registered with Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) as required and maintain the permit required for applicable transfers of personal data outside Tanzania. This Policy applies to information collected through the Marketplace, its accounts, listings, enquiries, reports and supporting documents.

We handle personal data under applicable law, including Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and applicable regulations. Other mandatory privacy rights may apply depending on where you live.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, account role, verification and account status, and account security or sign-in records.
  • Listing information: property title and description, property type, price, size, region and other location fields, map coordinates, photographs, seller name, listing status and dates.
  • Verification documents: files a seller or agent uploads to support a listing review, such as permitted title or property documents.
  • Marketplace activity: saved listings, enquiries, messages, visit dates, reports, report details and actions taken to review or resolve them.
  • Technical and security information: records needed to operate and protect the site, including request and error details held by service providers. The site also uses a pseudonymous browser token to help deduplicate listing views and limit repeated reports.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, one-time codes, or personal information about another person unless you have authority and it is necessary. Where a property document contains unrelated identity or contact details, redact them where practical before uploading.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; publish and manage listings; review supporting documents; enable enquiries and messages; process fraud reports; administer roles and verification status; prevent abuse; maintain site security; respond to requests; and meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. We use personal data only for purposes permitted by applicable law, and rely on consent where required.

4. Public listings and private documents

Listings with “verified” or “sold” status that are not flagged or removed can be read by the public. Public pages may show the listing title and description, price, property type, size, seller name, photographs and location details, including the map coordinates supplied for the property. Search engines may index this information. Do not publish a location or personal detail you do not want publicly available.

“Verified” is an internal platform review label; it is not an official finding of legal ownership or title. See the Terms of Use for the limits of that review.

Supporting land documents are stored separately in a private bucket. Access is restricted to the account that uploaded the document and authorised administrators. They are used for review and are not displayed as public listing files. Listing photographs, by contrast, may be publicly served with the listing.

5. Who receives information

We share information only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Other users: a seller or assigned agent receives enquiries and messages sent about their listing. Public listing information is available to site visitors and may be copied by search engines.
  • Service providers: Supabase provides database, authentication and file storage; Cloudflare serves and protects the website; jsDelivr supplies browser libraries; OpenStreetMap and Esri provide map tiles when a map is used. These providers may process technical connection information under their own privacy terms.
  • Authorities and advisers: we may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the Marketplace, or to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

We do not sell account or enquiry data. We do not currently process property purchase payments or run advertising on the Marketplace.

6. International processing

The Marketplace database is hosted by Supabase in the EU West 1 region. Cloudflare and other providers may process technical data in countries where they operate. This means personal data may be processed outside Tanzania. We maintain the PDPC permit required for applicable cross-border transfers and use the safeguards required by law. For information about a specific transfer or its safeguards, contact us.

7. Browser storage and security

The Marketplace uses browser storage to keep a signed-in session and a pseudonymous token used for view deduplication and report-abuse limits. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset that token. Your browser also communicates directly with hosting, authentication, storage and map providers when you use those features.

We use access controls and service-provider security measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and contact us promptly if you believe your account or personal information has been exposed.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate your account and listings, complete document review, handle enquiries and reports, protect the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention depends on the type of record and applicable requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it where reasonably possible. Backups and records kept for legal or security reasons may remain for a limited period.

To request account closure or deletion, email us. We will verify the request and handle it under applicable law. Some records may need to be retained where the law allows or requires it. Removing a public listing does not remove copies already cached by a search engine or another third party.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data we hold, access or correct it, request deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or exercise other rights available to you. To make a request, email lucasjames4363@gmail.com from an address we can use to verify your identity. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm the request and protect your account.

You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission. If you are outside Tanzania, you may have additional rights under local law.

10. Children

The Marketplace is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. Do not create an account or submit a child’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can review it.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when our services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top shows when the current version was published. Material changes will be highlighted on the Marketplace where appropriate.

Privacy questions, requests or complaints: lucasjames4363@gmail.com.

Additional information about Tanzanian data-subject rights and cross-border transfers is available from the PDPC.
